CPR distance recordings

Open a CSV from the logs folder. Data stays on this computer. The graph shows the raw sensor-to-pad gap, not compression depth: pressing toward the sensor decreases the distance.

Choose a recording to begin.
Move the pointer over the graph to inspect the nearest sample.

Blue: valid measurements. Red marks: invalid measurements. Lines break at invalid samples and gaps longer than 210 ms. No filtering or interpolation is applied. The validity flag means an echo passed the sketch’s range check; it does not prove a correct target or stroke. StrokeTest uses a narrower configured range than DistanceCapture.
