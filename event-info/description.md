# Description from Discord
Join us for the IEEE TAMU Technothon, where innovation meets impact as we tackle real-world challenges under the theme "Engineering for Disasters." Bring your creativity and technical skills to design solutions that could help communities prepare for, respond to, and recover from disasters!

# Notes from the Kickoff Event

## Schedule
Location: WEB 232

The event lasts from Friday, October 16 through Sunday, October 18.

These are the hours when the event is open, meaning officers are available to assist and you can checkout parts:

Day 1: Friday 6:00 PM - Saturday 12:00 AM
Day 2/3: Saturday 6:00 PM - Sunday 5:00 PM

This totals to 29 hours, but between the start and end times, there are 47 hours.

You are permitted to leave the event room at any time.

## Teams
Maximum of 4 people.
Members of a team must be finalized and registered by Friday 8:00 PM.

## Judging Criteria
+ Performance
+ Theme: The theme is disaster relief: climate, first responders, natural disasters, etc. It must relate to at least one of training, response, or prevention.
+ Presentation: During the judging period, your team explains and demonstrates your project to a club officer. This is where you are awarded points.

Judging period: Sunday 5:15 PM - 5:30 PM

Creativity will be rewarded, though it is not an explicit judging criteria. The project is not supposed to be advanced, but more so a proof-of-concept.

## Other
The intention is that projects are made on a breadboard, not with sautering, but this is not a hard rule at all.

Brunch provided on Sunday, at an unknown time.
