Texas A&M University students have free access to the Fab Lab, a place on campus with complimentary access to:

1. Neoden Solder stencil
2. Neoden Pick & place machine
3. Novastar solder reflow oven
4. Glowforge laser cutter
5. Carvera Desktop CNC Machine
6. Shop vacuum
7. Vice
8. PCB work station
9. Solder station
10. Electronics testing workbench: soldering station, oscilloscope, multimeter, hot air station, power supply
11. FDM printers
12. SLA printers
13. Wired network (for testing microcontroller Internet access)
14. Cricut Explore: cut and engrave various materials
15. Praxis PS 3D scanner
